## Foundation decisions — 2026-10-23

The following are design agreements, **not tested results**:
- Keep one illustrated visual bible across all games, with eight stable base colours and game-specific accents.
- Use Fraunces + Nunito Sans; give characters recognisable individual identities.
- Adaptive discovery leads to one approval package: concept, mechanics, brief and illustrated sample scenes.
- After approval, build independently via CLI and test functionality and narrative flow.
- Keep localisation separately editable and runtime builds as self-contained as practical.
